## 2. The owner's rules — verbatim, and binding

These were said in this and the previous sessions. Quote them back rather than reinterpreting them.

- „treffe niemals mehr solche Entscheidungen die spielentscheidend sind, du kannst es als Vorschlag
  bringen aber ich ersetze dich wenn du es nochmal ohne meine ausdrückliche Erlaubnis entscheidest"
- „hör auf design Entscheidungen zu treffen ohne das ich es absegnen"
- Skills simple: one effect per normal skill, the tiers are better versions of the same skill,
  **Episch has a small extra or is very strong**. Legendaries may do two things and must feel epic.
- „kein Freund von Deckel, lieber Werte niedriger" — no caps on skill ramps; the number per step is
  the regulator. The engine's crit-multiplier cap stays at 8 („Deckel bleibt", §7.22).
- **No direct score** from skills; faction score goes into the base, before the multipliers.
- „Raritäten dürfen nicht mit den genau gleichen Werten" — two tiers of one skill never share a value.
- „nur gierig messen" — the greedy evaluation is the one that counts; the random policy is history.
- „Schritt für Schritt abarbeiten" — one decision at a time, each one measured, documented, pushed.
- „schmiede so lassen", „glutbett lassen" — Schmiede and Glutbett stay as they are.
- Eis and Pflanze were to be ignored until their own round; **Pflanze is next after the skill pass**.

What this means in practice (`AGENTS.md`, *Decision authority*): every value, threshold, wording of a
player-visible text, mechanic, or removal is a **proposal** until the owner says yes. The agent
decides how it is built, how it is measured, and which technical regulator (`SIM_*` constants,
sweeps) is used to hit a target the owner set. Do not batch a design question into a message that
also reports a decision as done — the owner reads on a phone.

## 3. How a round runs

Every round so far has had the same shape; keep it.

1. The owner states a finding or a wish, often from the running game with a screenshot.
2. Diagnose in the sim before proposing (what carries, what is dead, why). Put the numbers into a
   new `### 7.x` section of `docs/skill-rework.md`, one or more **proposals with a recommendation**,
   nothing implemented.
3. Report in German, phone-screen length: finding, proposal, what it would cost. Then stop.
4. After the yes: implement in place (tier table, faction module, engine, texts, glossary, i18n,
   tests), measure again, complete the §7.x section („umgesetzt"), add a row to the
   **Änderungsprotokoll** table at the end of the spec, update the overview rows in §3.6 / §4.6 where
   a skill changed, run the gates, commit with the section number in the subject, push `exp`.
5. Report the measured effect, the watch points, and what remains open.

Commits: subject plus at most ~10 lines, English, section number in the subject
(`skills(exp): … (§7.25)`), the trailers required by the session prompt at the end. No model name
anywhere in the repository.

## 5. Measuring — tooling and reading rules

```bash
npm run sim -- --mode duel --runs 100                      # Feuer mono, Blitz mono, split, mix; parity = Floor Feuer ÷ Blitz
npm run sim -- --mode skills --runs 150 --explore 1000     # the greedy evaluation: explore → greedy → paired ablation per skill and tier
npm run sim -- --mode motor --runs 100                     # engine diagnosis: heat over the run, ionisation, score drivers
npm run sim -- --mode legendaries --runs 150 --explore 600 # every legendary granted mid-run, paired against the run without
ARCH=fire NOLEG=1 N=400 node sim/probes/lifts.mjs          # lift per skill in the pure faction build
node sim/probes/band.mjs                                   # balance band of the random policy (the guard test's numbers)
```

`sim/probes/README.md` lists the rest (streak, over-crit and cap-excess probes, `variant-duel.mjs`
for tier-table variants without touching the code, `print-skill-texts.mjs`, `gdoc.mjs` for the
owner's Google Doc from the `--mode skills` logs). Output of `npm run sim` lands in `sim/out/`
(git-ignored).

Reading rules, all measured in §7.15–§7.24:

- **The paired greedy ablation is the arbiter** for „carries" (Träger), „filler" (Füller), „hurts"
  (schadet) and „dead" (tot). Lifts (with ÷ without) are for legendaries and, with care, tier
  comparisons; for a skill held in 90 % of runs the „without" group is tiny. Always `NOLEG=1` for
  lifts, otherwise legendary holders distort the „without" group.
- **Explore tables vary ±20 % between runs**, hold rates of rarely held skills by ±30 points. A
  verdict on a skill under ~15 % hold rate needs two runs. Say „Streuung" when a number moved for that
  reason, and do not re-center anything on it.
- Parity between the factions is read in the duel: Floor (mono vs mono median), Mean, p90. Last stand
  §7.25: Floor 1,09×, Mean 0,70×, p90 0,52× — the tail is Blitz's.
- The balance guard `test/sim-balance-guard.test.js` holds bands around the random policy (seeds
  1..40). Re-center only after a change that moves the level on purpose, note it in §7.x, and never
  widen it to make a test green.
- Latest greedy stand (§7.25): median 77,8M, mean 176M, p90 398M, p95 803M, Ø 12,7 skills. Carriers:
  Sonnenkern, Resonanz, Doppelentladung, Klinge, Weißglut; Kettenblitz strong with Resonanz;
  Feuerlinie and Rückzündung are fillers; Blitzfänger dead in the greedy run; Donnergott noise.

## 7. Next task: the pass over all skills

Owner: „als nächstes machen wir einen pass über alle skills. ob alle unterschiedlich genug, genügend
Varianz, alle Beschreibungen gut sind". Three questions per faction, answered as a **report with
proposals first** — the owner decides skill by skill, „Schritt für Schritt".

**Method.** One table per faction with a row per skill: role (the „Rolle" column of §3.6 / §4.6),
trigger, resource in, resource out, what the greedy evaluation says (hold rate, ablation, §7.25),
and the nearest neighbour. Two skills are „too close" when trigger and output match and only the
number differs. Variance means the fourteen cover different triggers (per bar, per crit, per win,
per streak point, per formation, at a threshold, on loss, at round end) and different outputs
(charge, stacks, value, crit chance, crit multiplier, heat, score in the base, opponent debuff,
protection). Then read every tier text against `docs/text-style-guide.md` and
`docs/localization/i18n.md` — one sentence per effect, the ladder readable across the four tiers, no
parenthesis, established terms only (the skill-text-pass ledger in
`docs/workstreams/skill-text-pass/ledger.md` is the reference for tone and length).

**Candidate overlaps to look at first** (observed from the texts and the measurements, not decided):

- Blitz: Blitzableiter vs Reststrom (both „more bars"; Reststrom Episch is a bar-size change like
  Donnergott); Gewitterfront vs Entladung (same trigger, chance vs multiplier; the Gewitterfront
  Episch extra is Entladung's effect); Ladungsserie vs Vorentladung (same trigger, chance vs
  multiplier); Blitzfänger vs the passive permanent value vs Ionenfeld (three value sources — and
  Blitzfänger is dead in the greedy run); Kurzschluss vs Resonanz (both multiply stacks);
  Spannungsstau vs Blitzableiter Episch (both „win without crit"); Blitzschlag vs Kettenblitz (both
  depth motors); Serienschutz (the only protection; „schadet" in §7.6 and never a carrier since).
- Feuer: Feuersturm vs Rückzündung (both streak → score); Brandmal vs Lauffeuer vs Sonnenkern (three
  brands); Schmiede vs Damaststahl (near duplicates since §7.14); Glutstahl vs Feuerlinie (both value
  → score); Feuerwalze vs Glühende Klinge (both heat → value; Feuerwalze measured dead or noise in
  every evaluation, §7.6, §7.9, §7.13); Zunder vs
  Verbrennung Episch (heat ×1,5); Weißglut with Schmelzpunkt (check how „volle Leiste" reads when the
  bar is 200 — a rule question before a design one).

**Text checks to run**: `node sim/probes/print-skill-texts.mjs` for the texts; the longest ones are
Schmelzpunkt, Kurzschluss Episch, Serienschutz, Lauffeuer. Every text change needs
`npm run loc:export`, the glossary entry in `src/game/glossary.js` kept in step, and the text guards
in `test/skills.test.js` read before editing.

**Do not** strike below fourteen, touch Glutbett or Schmiede, add caps, add score outside the base,
add a glyph, icon or dependency, or change a value without the owner's yes. Where the pass ends in
value changes, measure them greedy (two runs where hold rates are low) and check the duel parity.

feat(icons): perk-selection emblems, on a measured render zone

PerkSelect.jsx had no icon code at all, so this builds the seam and fills
it. The 73 regular perks get the emblem of their category; the 21
legendary perks get their own. Two populations, two maps, no path between
them.

The render zone was MEASURED in the running application, which is what
scripts/skill-art-build.py was holding both lots back for: the bloom
radius is a CSS length divided by that width, so a borrowed number is
authoritative and wrong.

  perk tile          270.00 css-px
  emblem drawn at    265 x 201 css-px   <- the divisor
  1280 x 720         no <img> at all, below the 1400 px gate

The 5 px between the two is the tile's 4 px rarity edge plus 1 px on the
other sides, and this change got it wrong once: the first pass read the
tile off the grid and baked both lots against 270 (radius 22.76 against
the correct 23.18). Corrected and re-baked; the guard now rejects 270
alongside 277 and 270.66. Filed as ICONS-PERK-VIS-07.

Light alignment is per lot. perkcats applies the seven factors its README
already solved. legendaries needed 21 new ones, solved against total
emitted light rather than luminous area, because area-matching does not
converge on that lot: five of 21 files hit the solver ceiling and three
were still short of target there.

The legendary strip is centre-anchored where the category strip is
top-anchored. `center top` exists because the motifs put their statement
at the top; measured, that holds for the 7 category masters (light
centroid 0.42) and not for the 21 legendary ones (0.48). Top-anchored,
Henker keeps 34.7 % of its light and what survives is the axe shaft.
Deliberately one CSS class, so it stays cheap to reverse.

The skill lots re-bake byte-identical. `bake --lot corners` still
refuses; that zone belongs to icons-corners.

Guards: test/perk-art.test.js, 32 assertions, eleven seams each
counter-checked by deliberately breaking them.

V3, the human visual gate, was passed by the owner on 2026-08-22 —
verdict recorded verbatim in visual-review.md. That closes
ICONS-PERK-VIS-02 (the anchor) and ICONS-PERK-VIS-03 (the tile height).
It does not extend to what the captures could not show: 25 of the 28
emblems were never rendered by the application, only German, only DPR 1.

Gates: lint, build, VITE_PREVIEW=1 build, gen:db, loc:export (no diff)
all green. npm test 135 of 136 files; the one failure is a pre-existing
5000 ms timeout in test/i18n-guards.test.js, measured at HEAD as well.

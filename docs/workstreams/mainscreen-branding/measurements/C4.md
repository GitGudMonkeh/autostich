### 3.2 Fourteen desktop paddings left the JSX

They did not become tokens, and that is the correct outcome rather than a shortfall: §2c puts padding
that is not a box inset **outside the ladder** — *"a control pads against its label (which is why
`--btn-pad-*` sits outside the ladder), screen margins are layout."*

**Measured, so the claim is checkable:** `--btn-pad-y` is 10 px and `--btn-pad-x` 16 px, and **none of
the mainscreen's five buttons pads that way** — 16/24, 16, 20, 12/16, 16/24. The three ladder insets
(11 / 13 / 18) miss them too.

**What moving them buys is reachability, not a token.** A `dt:`-prefixed utility hits the desktop, where
the vocabulary applies, and no rule can reach it while it sits in a class list. `panel-tokens.test.js`
checks exactly that (the fourth of its four spellings — the half TYPO-12 missed), and it now covers this
screen.

**M4-F05 is that one row**, and it is reported rather than papered over. Its exposure is the smallest

### The second state measured nothing new, and that is itself the finding

The harness was built on the assumption that seeding a profile **past onboarding** would reveal the
other branch of the screen. Measured, it does not: `--fresh` (the survey's own seed) and the seeded
run return the **same 17 of 22**, at 357 surface nodes each. `GameOver.jsx` branches on
`onboarding`, which comes from `recordRun()`'s return value — **a property of the run, not of the
profile**. Seeding the profile cannot reach it; only playing an onboarding run can.

### M4-F06 — the harness lied once, and opening one image is what caught it

The contract asks for one owner-facing image to be **opened and confirmed**. That requirement paid
for itself immediately.

`--fresh` clears `localStorage` before seeding — a half-seeded profile is a different screen — and
clearing took `as_reset_epoch` with it. On the next boot `resetOnce()` (`storage.js:332`) found no
match, wiped, and `as_username` went with it: **the first-start modal stood over the end screen.**
The cell reported `ok · 371 surf · 17/22 selectors present` — cheerfully, because the end screen was
still behind the modal and every censused selector was still in the DOM.

Measured symptom: **371 surface nodes against the seeded run's 357.** Fixed by seeding the stamp;
re-measured at 357, and the opened image shows the end screen. **A harness that reports "ok" is not
a harness that measured the right screen**, and nothing but the eye would have caught this one.

### 3.2 M4-F01 — a prediction from M8 that the measurement refutes

M8 left `.go-*` carrying the glass gradient as a literal and wrote down what adopting the step would
cost:

> *"`--sf-glass` schreibt die Richtung aus (`180deg`), das Literal ließ sie weg. `to bottom` ist der
> Vorgabewert, also malt es dasselbe — die berechnete Zeichenkette lautet aber anders, und in einem
> Null-Delta-Lauf ist das ein Eintrag."*

**Measured: zero entries.** Six nodes per cell paint that gradient, and their computed
`background-image` is byte-identical on both sides:

```
before: linear-gradient(rgba(27, 26, 36, 0.93), rgba(22, 22, 32, 0.95))
after : linear-gradient(rgba(27, 26, 36, 0.93), rgba(22, 22, 32, 0.95))
```

The browser **normalises the default angle out of the computed value**, so writing `180deg` and
omitting it produce the same string. The observation was right — the two spellings differ in source —
and the prediction about what that costs was wrong. That is the fourth time in this round a document's
observations held and its predictions did not, and it is recorded here so the fifth worker does not
re-derive it.

### 3.3 M4-F04 — three `!important` fell, and the measurement is why

`#go-ruhe`'s guard asserted that `background`, `border` and `box-shadow` on `.go-actions > button`
must each carry `!important`, with the reason: *"as-edge-strong steht als Klasse und gewänne"*.

**That reason is false, and the cascade says so.** `.go-actions > button` weighs (0,1,1) against
`.as-edge-strong`'s (0,1,0) **and** sits later in the same unlayered sheet. It wins twice over.

Counter-check — the three declarations removed, rebuilt, the four harness cells re-measured against
the shipped build (`evidence/M4/counter-important/`):

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Compared | 4 cells, **1428 matched nodes** |
| Result | **ZERO computed deltas** |

And again at full scale, because the removal landed after the first survey run and the run had to be
repeated anyway: **160 cells, 25 189 matched nodes, ZERO deltas** between the build that carried the
three declarations and the build that does not (Part 6).

So they were removed. `color` and `padding` keep theirs and that is not an oversight: ink is not one
of the five axes and the inset sits outside the ladder, both are outside this task, and an
`!important` does not fall here without its own counter-check.

**Six `!important` had fallen in this round before this task; two were correctly kept. M4 makes it
ten and two** — three here and one at `.go-card .go-box`, whose border now goes through a redefinition
(`--ed-strong: var(--ed-quiet)`) exactly as M7 did at `.st-box`. That the redefinition reaches the
inline value is not argued but measured: the border computes to `rgb(42, 42, 52)`, which is
`--ed-quiet` — had the redefinition failed, `MENU_PANEL`'s inline `var(--ed-strong)` would have
resolved to the global `#302d40` instead.

### The counter-check found three defects in the new guard, and that is the point

Written down because "counter-check each" reads like a formality until it is not:

1. `indexOf(".as-edge-strong {")` also matched `.up-banner .as-edge-strong {` — a different rule,
   thousands of characters away, on the wrong side of the comparison.
2. `indexOf` reads the **first** definition; on a specificity tie the **last** one wins. A duplicate
   appended later would have been invisible. Now `lastIndexOf`.
3. `/!important/.test(btn)` asked whether *anything* in the rule carried `!important` — and
   `font-size`, `padding` and `color` still do, so the condition was permanently true and the order
   branch could never fire. Now asked **per property**, which is what the cascade asks.

All three were found by running the counter-check, not by reasoning about it.

observations held and their predictions did not.** So every number in both documents was re-measured

### 2.2 `erststart-redesign.md` — the two predictions that moved

**M9-F01 — the card is 900 × 444 in English and 900 × 429 in German.**

| | 1280×720 | 1400×700 | 1536×791 | 1600×900 | 1920×1080 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The document says *"die Kartengröße 900 × 444 px, die bei allen drei gemessenen Größen identisch ist"*.
**That is true and it is incomplete.** The size is identical across every viewport — the observation
holds exactly — but it is not identical across **languages**: German is 15.12 px shorter, because
`name.hint` wraps to two lines in English and one in German. 444 is the English figure, and English is
what a first-start screen shows by default, so the document measured the state it describes. The
number is not wrong; it is one of two. **Consequence:** the contract tunes in German, so the working
figure is 429, and both must be re-checked after the subline lands — it changes exactly this wrap.

**M9-F02 — the language control's conclusion is stale; its observation is not.**

The document says that once the divider overwrites the right button's state edge, *"übrig bleiben sechs
Punkte Flächenunterschied"* — that surface alone is left to carry the choice. Measured, both languages:

```
active    background rgb(25,25,34)   colour rgb(234,244,255)   box-shadow inset 0 -2px 0 rgb(38,198,230)
inactive  background rgb(19,19,24)   colour rgb(154,154,168)   box-shadow none
```

**The active half already carries a 2 px bottom edge in the deck colour** — which is precisely the
signal the document proposes to add (*"plus `inset 0 -2px 0` in der Deckfarbe"*). It was built by the
`#willkommen-ton` pass (`index.css:4717`). So what remains is not six points of surface; it is six
points of surface **and** the deck underline. The document's *direction* is right and already half
travelled — nothing it recommends is wrong in sign.

**Q3 — the subline landed, and it closed M9-F01 as a side effect.**


**Q4 — the panels carry the tint, and the segmented hits the design's number exactly.**

| | design asks | measured |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Part 3b — what moved that nobody asked for, named

**Three things moved that no document asked for. Each is named, and none of them is "while I was in
there".**

**1. Two button radii, 12 px to `--rd-md` (8).** `.un-first .un-save` and `.fb-send` carried a radius
with no step behind it. The acceptance gate requires every radius in these files to come from §2c, so
it could not stay a literal; and pulling it to the nearest step is the grip M8 already used for its
10 to 6 — *side by side, different radii read as different components*. Both buttons stand in a column
whose field, segmented and rows now all carry 8.

**2. The welcome title lost a `text-shadow` as well as its `filter`.** The design asks for *"kein
Schein"* and names the `drop-shadow`. `ty-display` stacks **three more** shadows in `currentColor`, and
those were invisible only because the gradient text was `color: transparent`. Giving the title an ink
colour switched them on — a white halo the numbers could not see, because `filter` correctly read
`none`. **Found by opening the capture, not by measuring it.**

**3. An infinite animation stopped.** With the reporter's card neutralised, `as-panel-sweep` was still
running on it — `background-position` moving across a background that no longer exists. It survived two
attempts: `.fb-card` (0,1,0) and `.fb-card.as-panel` (0,2,0) both lose to
`[data-skin="crt"] .as-panel.as-panel-deck` (0,3,0), and the reduced-effects exception at
`index.css:1581` is (0,5,0) with `!important` on top. Measured at each step rather than assumed — the
first attempt *looked* like it had worked, because the background **colour** had always been transparent.
